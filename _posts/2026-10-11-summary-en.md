**「Practical implication」** Producers modeling EPR fees or PCR supply should treat national recovery rates as geographically uneven. Limitations: the Ramsey County shift is a single-county grant example, and WasteZero's in-bag sortation of high-value items is a proposal, not a demonstrated result.

**「Next action」** Pull the Nature Communications Sustainability study for its MRF-proximity methodology, and track whether specific state EPR plans carry environmental-justice earmarks.
