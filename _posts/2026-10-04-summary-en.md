### [ADS recycled HDPE share falls to 38% as Georgia capacity ramps](https://resource-recycling.com/plastics/2026/10/01/ads-flexing-new-recycling-capacity-against-resin-spikes/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Advanced Drainage Systems \(ADS\) bought 388 million pounds of recycled plastic in fiscal 2026, 38% of resin purchases, down from 540 million pounds and 46% in fiscal 2025—the lowest share in five years, per its sustainability report. ADS targets 1 billion pounds annually by fiscal 2032 and says it is pivoting back toward recycled resin after a $30 million Cordele, Georgia, recycling expansion ramped in June, citing volatile virgin polyolefin pricing linked to supply constraints from the US-Israel war in Iran.

rss · Resource Recycling — markets and policy · Oct 1, 10:00

**「Why it matters」** Pipe and HDPE buyers: ADS’s procurement swing signals recycled color HDPE demand can move with virgin polyolefin prices, affecting bale pricing and availability; ADS says it buys about 30% of US color HDPE bales.

**「Practical implication」** Recycled HDPE feedstock buyers may see short-term color bale pricing pressure when large vertically integrated buyers ramp capacity; May color HDPE bale prices averaged 15.38 cents/lb, nearly double the prior month, held 11–13 cents/lb in June–July, then fell 42% in August. ADS reprocesses 57% of its recycled plastic internally; partners supply the rest.
