### [US PET recycling rate falls to 29.0% as RPET imports hit 39.1% share](https://resource-recycling.com/plastics/2026/11/11/pet-imports-reshape-us-markets-as-demand-grows/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

NAPCOR's 2025 data show the US PET bottle recycling rate fell a second year, to 29.0% from 30.2%, while average recycled content in US bottles rose to a record 22.9% \(revised 20.7% prior\) and the US/Canadian bottle-to-bottle share reached 69%. RPET sales to US and Canadian end markets grew 5.6%, but imports supplied all of that growth, hitting a record 39.1% of consumption \(up 3.6 points\). US bottle collection fell 3.6% to 1.821 billion pounds.

rss · Resource Recycling — markets and policy · Nov 11, 11:01

**「Why it matters」** Recycled-content targets now hinge more on domestic food-grade feedstock than on demand: NAPCOR ties lost washline reclamation capacity to rising import reliance, shifting PCR sourcing risk and compliance planning.

**「Practical implication」** Sourcing and qualification plans that assume domestic RPET availability may need import-capable supply. NAPCOR cites record-low bale prices and plant closures; the survey carries no state-level data, and EPR effects are too early to observe.

**「Next action」** Ask RPET suppliers to document feedstock origin and reclaimer capacity, then check that against your PCR-content specification.

rss · Resource Recycling — markets and policy · Nov 12, 11:00
