### [Dow signals PE price momentum into 2027](https://resource-recycling.com/plastics/2026/10/23/dow-bullish-on-pe-pricing-into-27/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On its Oct. 22 Q3 call, Dow said polyethylene pricing momentum should extend into 2027, citing higher feedstock costs, tighter inventories and war-related production constraints. Dow cited a cumulative 12 cents/lb in nominated October–November PE contract increases, a 5 cents/lb September contract rise and a 6 cents/lb weekly spot rise; ACC September data it cited showed inventory down 5 days of supply and 400 million lb.

rss · Resource Recycling — markets and policy · Oct 23, 12:00

**「Why it matters」** For North American PE buyers and packaging converters, the mechanism is ethane/naphtha cost pass-through plus inventory tightness into contract prices; Dow said every 3 cents/gal ethane rise requires about 1 cent/lb PE, with Q4 guidance assuming ethane in the mid-high 20s cents/gal.

**「Practical implication」** Treat the 12 cents/lb as nominated, not settled; grade, geography and settlement timing still determine realized cost, and Dow’s outlook is company commentary. Packaging cost plans may need an October–November and 2027 scenario, but the supplied material does not establish volume effects or customer acceptance.

**「Next action」** Track ACC inventory data and confirm whether October–November PE nominations settle with suppliers.
