rss · Resource Recycling — markets and policy · Sep 25, 16:54

**「Why it matters」** EPR fee exposure on plastic packaging in Oregon now turns on appellate review; a published Ninth Circuit decision would bind the parallel California SB 54 challenge \(Nebraska v. Heller, 17 states\) in the same circuit.

**「Practical implication」** Expect Oregon fee exposure to continue: Ninth Circuit civil appeals have historically taken over a year, and Circular Action Alliance's \(CAA\) fee methodology stays confidential. NAW cites CAA's 2025 report showing $145.5M collected against $56.5M spent; Simon read the gap as reserve-building, not a violation.

**「Next action」** Track the Nebraska v. Heller injunction hearing set for January 2027, which may yield a California ruling before the Ninth Circuit decides; confirm CAA fee schedules and membership terms applying to your packaging.
