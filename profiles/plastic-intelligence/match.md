# Plastic intelligence profile

Use this profile for timely, credible developments that could change a decision in the plastics value chain: market demand, resin economics, sustainable materials, recycling technology, material performance, emerging technology, supply and feedstocks, regulation, standards, research, patents, startups, investment, or industrialization.

Prefer original regulations, standards bodies, peer-reviewed papers, patents, official company announcements with technical detail, credible trade reporting, and price information with a stated geography, grade, unit, and period. Exclude generic sustainability promotion, consumer recycling tips, unsupported environmental claims, minor product launches, duplicate wire copy, and stories where plastics are incidental.
