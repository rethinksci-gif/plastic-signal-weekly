    METHOD

    Built from the published issues each time a new one goes out. Drivers, polymers and regions come from a fixed keyword list matched against each item's title, summary and tags (the AI-written tags alone are too varied to count reliably). Momentum compares each theme's share of items, so a quiet week does not read as a decline.
